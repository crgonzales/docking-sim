The v0.8.0 shell/billboard renderer's night-lights, relief-normal and cloud textures, the seeded cloud coverage mask and the ESO starfield panorama were removed in v0.14.2 with that renderer; the library pipeline lights the Blue Marble day imagery and water mask through the composer, and volumetric weather comes from `clouds/volumetric/`.

Conventions for future model drops: meter scale, pivot at vehicle COM, target docking port faces −ŷ, chaser docking axis +ŷ; record the normalization transform (scale, rotation, pivot offset) in this table. Dragon is the active chaser. The old ESAS model and station GLTF remain unused.

## Dragon 2 attribution and registration

“SpaceX - Dragon 2” by **KUBAHA**, available at the listing above, is licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0**. Runtime modifications: normalize scale/port datum, open only the nose cover, and adjust cloned surface materials for the game lighting. Geometry and source animation data are retained unchanged. No SpaceX endorsement is implied.

The mesh contains twelve side RCS mouths plus four around the docking ring. Their locations and axes are registered in `packages/sim-core/src/crewDragon.ts`. Side axes come from the circular inner faces; mouth points sit at the furthest lip projection along each axis. Top mouths face +Y. This is registration to an artist's model, not manufacturer propulsion data. The demo retains its 25 N jets, 976 kg dry mass and original propellant/inertia tuning; it is not a flight-accurate Crew Dragon simulation.

## F/A-18C attribution

The acquired Hornet source is the creator-listed [McDonnell Douglas F/A-18C Hornet by Rhine_Lab_Muelsyse](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/mcdonnell-douglas-fa-18c-hornet-c68c8417c8e84864b2a5e0c35c178fd9), redistributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The creator, source listing and license remain recorded in the embedded GLB metadata and this manifest.

Runtime changes are non-destructive: the source hierarchy, geometry and textures are cached, each FLIGHT mount clones the hierarchy, and only per-instance material clones are owned and disposed. The adapter preserves glTF root transforms, maps its axes into body FRD, normalizes its measured length, and presents a clean airborne configuration by hiding deployed gear, doors, hook, pylons and tank. Source control groups remain neutral static geometry; the procedural Hornet is retained as the loading/error fallback. No cockpit fidelity claim is made.
