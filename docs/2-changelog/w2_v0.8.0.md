## Changes

- **Sky config**: single source of truth in `sky/skyConfig.ts` — physical inputs (radii, altitudes, scattering coefficients, camera envelopes) with all renderer values derived in code and pinned by `skyConfig.test.ts` oracles; per-layer magic constants across Earth/Clouds/VolumetricClouds eliminated
- **Atmosphere**: Hillaire-style precomputed transmittance (256×64) and multiple-scattering (32×32) LUTs baked by `bakeAtmosphere.mjs` from real Rayleigh/Mie/ozone coefficients (artifacts committed, bake deterministic); 12-step raymarch shell with HG(g=0.8), exponential tone-map rolloff, tangent-chord clamp at the planet silhouette; aerial perspective on the surface from the same LUTs with a thick-path limb continuity term; sun-light and sun-sprite extinction tint from the transmittance LUT. Two latent defects fixed en route: RGB float LUT textures are unfilterable in WebGL2 (silently sampled as zero — the shell had never rendered) and the multiple-scattering term was double-attenuated by sun transmittance
- **Clouds**: EVE-style stack — flat deck (7 km) + cirrus (12.7 km) + 12,000-instance volumetric band (1.5–9 km) placed by seeded rejection sampling against a regenerated grayscale coverage mask (sRGB-decoded to match GPU sampling); shared coverage transfer function keeps deck, shadows, and puffs identical; equirectangular UV convention corrected across all layers (shadows/volumetrics were 180° from the deck); instance-field frustum culling disabled (unit-plane bounds blinked the whole layer); vertex-computed footprint fades stop sub-pixel puffs and FBM octaves from shimmering
- **Surface**: GEBCO-derived relief normal map (land-masked, day/glint terms only), ocean tint + fresnel rim + glint with wave normals gated to near-water range (invisible from orbit, reserved for future descent), night-map mip bias against city-light flicker; Earth/shell/deck spheres at 192 segments
- **Sun**: camera-relative anchor at the derived debug-envelope distance (≈60,000) with correct 0.53° angular size — no parallax at any zoom
- **Assets**: NASA Blue Marble day/night/spec/cloud + GEBCO topography as KTX2/UASTC with full mip chains, provenance (URLs + SHA-256) in `ASSETS.md`; normalized glTF chaser/target committed but `USE_GLTF_MODELS` stays false — owner judged the baked hull texture too dark; rework deferred
- **UX**: debug camera (`B`, 2–40,000 range), arrow-key orbit + PgUp/PgDn zoom at a 30 Hz camera tick, FPS counter in the mode bar
- **Verification**: 145 tests green (105 sim-core, 14 scenario, 26 web); GPU screenshot checkpoints (nadir, limb, terminator, night, full-disc, flight zoom) at 60 fps; temporal frame-diff loop used to hunt shimmer to <0.1% of frame pixels
