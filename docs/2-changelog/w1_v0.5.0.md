## Changes

- **QP solver** (`qp.ts`): pure-TS dense primal active-set QP with explicit feasible-start validation, deterministic pivoting, and a status enum — verified by KKT stationarity/feasibility/complementarity oracles and hand-solved problems.
- **Corridor & envelope** (`corridor.ts`): the 10°/15° approach cones and capture envelope as shared spec-linked constants with closed-form-tested geometry — MPC, monitors, truth contact evaluation, and the HUD all read the same numbers.
- **MPC** (`mpc.ts`, `authority.ts`): 1 Hz condensed CW MPC in specific-force variables; reference-preselected corridor facets (convexity preserved), soft corridor + terminal constraints with unit-normalized slacks and a by-construction feasible start; acceleration authority as an inscribed octahedron probed against a scratch allocator with torque reserve required; graceful 10 Hz LQR fallback on non-optimal status or degenerate authority. The **headline test: MPC flies 250 m to a DOCKED outcome with the truth capture envelope green at contact.**
- **Monitors & abort** (`monitors.ts`): two-level corridor response (recoverable caution vs 15 s sustained / hard outer-cone abort trigger), engagement-range gating; passive abort = closed-loop safing burn (CW non-drift + opening bias) then rate-damped coast — proven by a 2-orbit keep-out oracle (range ≥ 0.8× abort epoch).
- **Outcomes** (`sim.ts`): truth-side port-to-port contact detection; capture evaluated against TRUTH with **full-attitude misalignment** (roll included); DOCKED pins the complete pose (re-pinned every tick against LVLH rotation); SimLoop is the sole outcome arbitrator (contact before abort, first latch immutable); `commandAbort()` public command.
- **Manual-rate tuning**: impulse-carry reset on torque-sign reversal fixes the v0.4.2 over-damped rotation — full stick now reaches ≥80% of 1.5°/s within 3 s (truth-coupled acceptance test); manual-coupling regression stays <1°.
- **Web**: mouse is camera-only — right-drag orbits, wheel zooms (per-mode state, Hill-frame by documented design); `V` cycles PID/LQR/MPC live, `Backspace` aborts; keybinds overlay (`H`/`?`) rendered from the single-source bindings table with a completeness test; outcome banners (CAPTURE CONFIRMED / CONTACT OUTSIDE ENVELOPE / ABORT — SAFE COAST); C&W tiles live (CTRL corridor caution/abort, GUID MPC fallback).
- **Review catches of note**: roll-blind capture check, far-range instant-abort, docked-attitude drift, force-only authority probing, and a crash path on degenerate authority — all found by code review and fixed; during implementation the reviewer also caught an early pass introducing a 180°-flipped docking convention and engineering tests around it (fixed to the established geometry before review).
