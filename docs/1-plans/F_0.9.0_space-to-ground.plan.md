# Space-to-Ground Continuous World Implementation Plan

## Overview

v0.9.0 makes the planet a place, not a backdrop: seamless free-camera flight
from the 40,000 km debug envelope down to standing on the surface, with no cut,
no loading screen, and no scene swap. Quadtree (PQS-style) terrain LOD driven by
NOAA ETOPO 2022 elevation data plus procedural detail synthesis and curated
~10 m USGS DEMs for the SpaceX sites (Cape Canaveral/KSC and Starbase Boca
Chica), the Hillaire atmosphere adapted for viewing from inside, the cloud stack
traversable, and KSP-style animated near-field water. This is the world
foundation the SpaceX-style booster-landing feature (next release) flies
through; no new vehicle physics ships here — `sim-core` and `scenario` are
untouched.

## Problem Statement

Three hard blockers stand between v0.8.0 and a camera on the ground:

1. **Precision.** The scene runs two coexisting scales (Hill content at 1 unit
   = 1 m near origin; the Earth group at 1 unit = 1 km, positioned so angular
   size is preserved). The angular-size trick is exact for distant viewing but
   diverges as the camera approaches the surface — the two worlds disagree
   about parallax, and no camera path can "land." Worse, float32 at magnitude
   6371 (km units) has ~0.76 m of vertex precision — visible jitter at human
   scale even if the scales were unified naively.
2. **Resolution.** The Earth is a single 192-segment textured sphere with a 4k
   normal map — ~10 km/px at the equator; even the underlying 21,600-px GEBCO
   source is only ~1.9 km/px. There is no geometry below those scales.
3. **View direction.** The atmosphere raymarch assumes the camera is outside
   the shell; the cloud deck is a one-sided textured sphere with no underside;
   ocean waves are gated OFF below orbit precisely because the current pattern
   is continental-scale fiction.

## Solution Architecture

Five subsystems, dependency-ordered:

1. **Unified world frame + floating origin (`worldFrame.ts`).** One scale
   everywhere: 1 scene unit = 1 metre (`SKY_CONFIG.renderScaleMPerUnit` 1000 →
   1; every derived value follows automatically through `SKY_DERIVED`). All
   world-anchored logical positions are kept in float64 on the CPU (JS numbers)
   as `[x, y, z]` metre triples; a render-origin anchor is subtracted in f64
   *before* casting to f32 for the GPU, so the GPU only ever sees small
   magnitudes (Cesium's RTC method). When the camera drifts beyond a rebase
   threshold from the anchor, the anchor snaps to the camera and every
   top-level object position is re-derived — one uniform/position update, no
   vertex data touched, invisible by construction (verified by an invariance
   oracle). The logarithmic depth buffer (already on) covers the z-range.
   Existing camera-relative content (sun sprite) and `planetCenter`-uniform
   shaders (atmosphere, surface, clouds) already parameterize the right things
   — they get new per-frame values, not new logic. The `stepLength * 1000.0`
   km→m conversions inside shaders become config-derived (and equal 1).

2. **Terrain quadtree (`scene/terrain/`).** Cube-sphere with 6 root faces;
   nodes split on a screen-space-error metric until patches reach ~1 m
   effective resolution near the camera. Each leaf renders a 33×33-vertex
   patch: unit-cube-face grid → spherified direction → radially displaced by
   the height function; vertices are generated in f64 **relative to the patch
   centre** and stored f32 (per-patch RTC — this is what makes ground-level
   precision work). Skirts (KSP's own choice) hide LOD cracks — no stitching.
   Patch meshes are generated in a Web Worker pool (the `monteCarloWorker`
   strided-pool pattern) and transferred as ArrayBuffers; the render loop never
   builds geometry. Every patch gets a real bounding sphere and horizon
   culling — the v0.8.0 InstancedMesh frustum-culling bug is the cautionary
   tale, and a test pins patch bounds to their vertices.

   **Height function** (pure TS, worker-shared, oracle-tested):
   `base(lat, lon) = etopo(lat, lon) + detail(lat, lon) · mask`
   `height(lat, lon) = mix(base, heroDem(lat, lon), heroWeight(lat, lon))`
   where `heroWeight` is 0 outside a hero region, 1 in its interior, and ramps
   smoothly across the feather band — `heroDem` is an ABSOLUTE elevation that
   replaces (never adds to) the base inside its region.
   - **Encoding & datum contract:** all tiles store signed metres relative to
     mean sea level in Mapbox-style terrain-RGB PNG
     (`h = offsetM + (R·65536 + G·256 + B) · scaleM`, with codec values in
     each manifest; hero DEMs use `offsetM=-10000, scaleM=0.1`, while the
     base pyramid uses `offsetM=-11000, scaleM=1`) — browsers quantize 16-bit
     grayscale PNG to 8 bits through canvas, so terrain-RGB is the standard
     lossless-through-canvas encoding; decode is a pure tested function. A
     reserved RGB value encodes no-data (sampler substitutes the parent-level
     sample). USGS 3DEP heights (NAVD88) are treated as MSL-compatible — the
     geoid/ellipsoid distinction is deliberately out of scope for a spherical
     planet model and documented as such in `heightField.ts`.
   - `base` raster: the pyramid is built from **NOAA ETOPO 2022 60-arc-second
     ice-surface GeoTIFF** (21600×10800, native float32 metres, tiled/DEFLATE, public
     domain — decode verified with `geotiff`, including TIFF predictor 3) rather than the cached 8-bit GEBCO visualization PNG — the PNG is
     a rendered relief image whose byte→metre mapping is undocumented lossy
     quantization (~77 m steps; its existing use in `makeEarthNormal.mjs` is
     fine for normals, wrong for elevations). Native float32 metres remove the
     encoding guesswork entirely; `geotiff` applies the native float32 TIFF
     predictor while decoding the GeoTIFF. Resolutions, stated correctly: source 21600 px ≈ 1.9 km/px at
     the equator; the served pyramid top level is capped at 10800×5400 ≈
     3.7 km/px. The bake asserts resolution-appropriate ground-truth checks:
     an Everest window maximum above +7 km, a Mariana window minimum below
     −10 km, and an open-ocean coastline crossing 0.
   - `detail`: seeded fractal value-noise octaves (deterministic, same seed
     convention as cloud placement), amplitude modulated by local base slope
     and the land/ocean mask so plains stay flat, mountains get ridged, and the
     ocean floor gets nothing. Provides believable relief from the ~4 km base
     scale down to ~1 m — the same division of labour KSP uses (coarse real
     base + procedural character).
   - `heroDem`: absolute high-res DEM for two curated regions: Cape
     Canaveral/KSC (≈28.6°N 80.6°W) and Starbase Boca Chica (≈26.0°N 97.2°W)
     — USGS 3DEP 1/3-arc-second (~10 m, public domain), read from pinned
     cached files + tiled by script with URL/SHA-256 provenance in `ASSETS.md`, same discipline as
     GEBCO/clouds.

   **Tile ownership (`terrain/tileSource.ts`):** `heightField` stays pure and
   synchronous — it samples a *resident tile set*; all async I/O lives in a
   dedicated tile source: manifest-driven fetch, in-flight request
   deduplication, LRU eviction with a byte budget, and stale-request dropping
   on eviction. The quadtree may only split a node once the tiles its children
   sample are resident (split-gating, so patches never build from missing
   data); a non-resident sample falls back to the finest resident ancestor
   level. Workers hold NO persistent tile cache: each build request carries
   the few tiles its patch samples (request-scoped structured clone, bounded
   by construction), so the main tile-source LRU is the single residency
   authority — if profiling shows clone overhead, the documented upgrade path
   is a small per-worker LRU with its own explicit byte cap in config, never
   an unbounded copy. The camera collision floor uses the finest resident
   sample; above the
   engagement altitude the floor is inactive by construction, so pre-arrival
   readiness is a preload of the destination's coarse levels, not a race.

   **Far/near continuity:** the existing textured globe remains the far
   representation. Below a config-derived engagement altitude the quadtree
   fades in over the globe (both render, terrain alpha ramps up over an
   altitude band); the globe's own relief normal map means the two agree at the
   crossfade distance. No pop, no swap — the KSP scaled-space/local-space
   handoff, done as a crossfade.

   **Texturing:** procedural splat by slope/altitude/latitude (sand, scrub,
   rock, snow) modulated by the existing 4k day albedo for regional colour, and
   detail noise to break tiling. No imagery dependency in this release.

3. **Atmosphere from inside (`sky/` shaders).** The shell raymarch gains the
   camera-inside branch: integration starts at the camera (`max(0, entry)`)
   instead of the shell entry point. Clamping alone is NOT sufficient — 12
   uniform samples give ~8 km zenith and ~94 km horizon steps from the ground,
   skipping the dense lower atmosphere entirely — so the inside branch also
   switches to density-warped sampling: sample distances distributed by an
   exponential warp matched to the Rayleigh scale height (concentrating steps
   near the camera/low altitudes) with the count raised (config-derived, ~32)
   when the camera is below the TOA. The warp is implemented once in shared
   GLSL + mirrored in `atmosphereMath.ts`, and an oracle test pins the warped
   12/32-step integral against a 1024-step direct reference within tolerance
   for zenith, horizon, and sunset geometries. From the ground the same
   integral then renders the full daytime sky dome, twilight, and night. Terrain patches get true
   LUT-driven aerial perspective using the standard transmittance ratio
   `T(camera→point) = T(point→TOA, dir) / T(camera→TOA, dir)` (Bruneton),
   added to the shared sky GLSL — this replaces the orbit-calibrated
   `thickPath` approximation on terrain (the far globe keeps it; they agree at
   the crossfade). A smooth altitude-driven exposure adaptation (config curve,
   not a hidden constant) spans the brightness gap between space views
   (calibrated at `ATMOSPHERE_INTENSITY` 26) and the much brighter ground-level
   sky.

4. **Traversable clouds (`Clouds.tsx`, `VolumetricClouds.tsx`).** The deck
   gains an underside: the deck material (currently default `FrontSide` —
   invisible from below regardless of shading) switches to `DoubleSide`, and
   the fragment shader branches on `gl_FrontFacing`: back faces get the
   lit-from-above base treatment (darker, transmittance-tinted) while front
   faces keep the current look; passing through the layer runs a short screen
   fog ramp driven by the shared coverage function sampled at the camera
   position — the same transfer function
   everywhere, per the v0.8.0 registration lesson. Volumetric puffs fade out
   softly when the camera is within a puff radius (alpha ramp on
   camera-to-centre distance) so flying through one is a whiteout-and-clear,
   not a billboard flip. Placement stays world-anchored and deterministic; two
   additional static caps are placed over the hero regions so descents there
   have weather (the orbital cap covers the Hill corridor already).

5. **Free-fly camera + near water (`viewStore.ts`, `manualControls.ts`,
   ocean shading).** DEBUG mode gains a FLY submode (toggle key): WASD+mouse
   free flight whose speed scales with height above terrain (AGL from the
   height function — the CPU-side sampler doubles as the camera's collision
   floor, clamped ~2 m above ground). W/A/S/D currently command spacecraft
   pitch/yaw, so FLY takes explicit input precedence in the bindings layer:
   entering FLY suppresses and zeroes all manual spacecraft commands, held-key
   state is cleared on every submode transition (both directions), and the
   keybinds overlay reflects the active owner of the keys. ORBIT submode
   stays as-is. Near-field
   water: the reserved `OCEAN_WAVE_FADE` gate finally gets its real content —
   multi-octave animated normal waves + specular sized for human scale, active
   below a few km, on sea-level ocean patches (height ≤ 0 renders the water
   surface at the geoid radius with its own material). No displacement — a
   shading-only ocean, KSP-style.

## Implementation Details

### 1. World frame

**Files**: `apps/web/src/scene/worldFrame.ts` (new),
`apps/web/src/scene/sky/skyConfig.ts`, `apps/web/src/scene/SceneRoot.tsx`,
`apps/web/src/scene/Earth.tsx`, `Clouds.tsx`, `VolumetricClouds.tsx`,
`SunSprite.tsx`, `Starfield.tsx`, `CameraRig.tsx`,
`DockingCameraPiP.tsx` (the PiP camera and its look target position
themselves from Hill coordinates and must consume the render origin like the
main rig — an easy-to-miss second camera), `viewStore.ts`.

- `worldFrame.ts`: f64 anchor state + `toRender(positionF64): [x,y,z]`
  subtraction helpers + rebase logic (threshold config-derived); pure TS math
  kept import-clean for direct unit testing.
- `skyConfig.ts`: `renderScaleMPerUnit: 1`; audit every consumer of
  `SKY_DERIVED` scene values; camera envelopes (`flightMaxOrbit`,
  `debugMaxOrbit`, orbit limits in `viewStore`) re-derived — same physical
  distances, metre numbers.
- Shaders: remove hardcoded `* 1000.0` unit conversions in the atmosphere
  raymarch/ozone terms in favour of a config-injected metres-per-unit factor
  (which is now 1); `planetCenter`/anchor uniforms updated per frame from f64.
- **Gate**: all four v0.8.0 checkpoint framings (nadir, limb, full-disc,
  flight zoom) re-captured and visually identical before any terrain work.

### 2. Terrain data pipeline

**Files**: `apps/web/scripts/makeBaseTiles.mjs` (new),
`apps/web/scripts/makeHeroDem.mjs` (new),
`apps/web/public/assets/terrain/` (new asset tree),
`apps/web/public/assets/ASSETS.md`.

- Base pyramid (`makeBaseTiles.mjs`): read and checksum-verify the cached
  ETOPO 2022 60″ ice-surface GeoTIFF (pinned URL + SHA-256; this offline script
  never fetches); decode native float32 metres via `geotiff` (including TIFF
  predictor 3); resample to the
  10800×5400-capped top level and emit terrain-RGB PNG tiles (256×256) + a
  JSON manifest (levels, tile size, bounds, no-data value); deterministic
  output, byte-identity test like the LUT bake; resolution-appropriate
  ground-truth elevation assertions (Everest/Mariana/coastline) run in the
  script.
- Hero DEMs: read and checksum-verify the cached USGS 3DEP 1/3″ tiles covering
  both regions (pinned URLs + SHA-256; the Boca Chica region mosaics both
  cached tiles across the 26°N seam; this offline script never fetches),
  crop/resample to terrain-RGB region tiles + manifest (same encoding
  contract); record provenance and the public-domain licence rows in
  `ASSETS.md`.
- Budget (arithmetic, then measured): capped top level is 10800×5400×3 B
  ≈ 175 MB *uncompressed*; terrain-RGB PNG compresses smooth bathymetry
  heavily — target ≤ 60 MB on disk for the full pyramid, enforced by the
  script printing per-level sizes and failing the build if the total exceeds
  the budget (drop the finest level to comply). Hero regions ≤ ~40 MB
  combined. Tiles are fetched on demand — resident decoded bytes are governed
  by the single main-thread tile-source LRU budget (workers hold only
  request-scoped copies, bounded per build).

### 3. Quadtree + height function

**Files**: `apps/web/src/scene/terrain/quadtree.ts` (new, pure),
`terrain/heightField.ts` (new, pure), `terrain/terrainWorker.ts` (new),
`terrain/TerrainPatches.tsx` (new), `terrain/terrainShaders.ts` (new),
`terrain/index.ts` (new).

- `quadtree.ts`: cube-face node addressing, split/merge from camera position +
  screen-space-error threshold, neighbour/skirt metadata; pure and
  deterministic — direct oracle tests.
- `heightField.ts`: the three-layer height function + AGL sampler; pure; shared
  by workers (patch build), the camera floor, and tests.
- `terrainWorker.ts`: pool on the `monteCarloWorker` pattern; builds
  position/normal/uv/skirt buffers per patch relative to the patch centre;
  transfers ArrayBuffers.
- `TerrainPatches.tsx`: owns the live node set, requests builds, swaps patches
  only when children are ready (no holes), sets real bounding spheres +
  `frustumCulled` correctly, applies per-frame RTC positions from
  `worldFrame`, disposes retired patches (v0.8.0 leak lesson).
- `terrainShaders.ts`: splat + shared sky lighting + LUT aerial perspective
  (ratio trick) + cloud-shadow reuse via the shared coverage GLSL.

### 4. Sky, clouds, water, camera

**Files**: `Earth.tsx` (atmosphere + far-globe crossfade), `sky/lighting.ts`
(aerial-ratio GLSL + exposure curve), `Clouds.tsx` (underside + fog ramp),
`VolumetricClouds.tsx` (near-puff fade, hero caps), `sky/cloudPlacement.ts`
(multi-cap placement), `viewStore.ts` + `input/manualControls.ts` +
`input/bindings.ts` (FLY submode, AGL speed scaling, collision floor),
`Earth.tsx` ocean/`terrainShaders.ts` water material (near waves),
`hud/KeybindsOverlay.tsx` (new keys).

## Technical Considerations

- **Pattern Usage**: single-source `SKY_CONFIG` + derivations (all new
  altitudes/thresholds/budgets live there); worker pool per `monteCarloWorker`;
  seeded determinism per cloud placement; shared GLSL modules; provenance
  discipline per `ASSETS.md`.
- **Package Boundary**: everything in `apps/web`. `sim-core`/`scenario`
  untouched. `heightField`/`quadtree`/`worldFrame` are pure TS inside the app
  (renderer-adjacent, not flight software — the booster feature will get its
  own sim-core terrain-height port with its own oracle, by design not shared
  with the render path).
- **Conventions**: **units convention changes** — the render scene becomes
  uniformly 1 unit = 1 m with a camera-relative floating origin; ARCHI.md
  Conventions must be updated at release (the old two-scale note dies).
  Equirect UV convention unchanged and load-bearing for base/hero tile
  addressing (same `cloudSphericalUv` mapping).
- **Determinism**: procedural detail noise fully seeded; quadtree split state
  is a pure function of camera position; workers are build-order-independent
  (patch content depends only on node address).
- **Performance**: 30 fps floor at ground level (owner decision, recorded in
  ARCHI); 60 fps expected in orbit views (no regression — terrain is dormant
  above the engagement altitude). Patch budget, worker count, and split
  threshold are config values tuned against the FPS counter.
- **Edge Cases**: cube-face seams and corners (patch addressing + skirt
  coverage at face boundaries); poles (cube-sphere avoids the classic pole
  singularity but face corners get oracle tests); date-line wrap in tile
  addressing; hero-region boundary feathering (no visible wall); rebase during
  worker builds (patch centres are absolute — RTC math immune by
  construction); camera below terrain (clamped by the AGL floor); ETOPO ocean
  depths (negative heights → water surface, not underwater terrain render).

## Performance & Cost Impact

Terrain is the largest render system yet: target ≤ ~300 live patches,
≤ ~700k terrain triangles, worker-built (zero main-thread geometry), dormant
above the engagement altitude so orbit/docking scenes are unaffected. New
assets add roughly ≤ 100 MB served (measured against budgets in the scripts;
level-capped if exceeded). 30 fps floor at ground level on the owner's
hardware; 60 fps preserved in orbit.

## Backward Compatibility

Docking gameplay, HUD, scenario mode, and Monte Carlo are untouched. The unit
unification is render-internal; telemetry and sim-core interfaces are
unchanged. v0.8.0 visual output must be pixel-comparable after Phase A — the
crossfade and terrain only activate below the engagement altitude, which no
existing camera path reaches by default.
