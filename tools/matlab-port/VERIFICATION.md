## Scope of the evidence

This verifies numerical consistency and independent physics invariants for
our model. Mass, inertia, jet performance and mesh-derived jet geometry are
game parameters, not validated Crew Dragon engineering data. CW translation
assumes a circular reference orbit and small relative separation. Rigid-body
inertia remains fixed while propellant changes mass. The plotted LQR solution
uses exact state and unsaturated force demand; it is separate from the pulsed
six-DOF experiments and does not demonstrate closed-loop thruster docking.
Sensors, estimators, FSW/MPC, contact logic and the F_0.16 SIL bridge are not
ported by this increment. Public usage and interfaces are in `README.md` and
`API.md`.
